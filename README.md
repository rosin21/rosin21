## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a Computational and Applied Mathematics graduate from Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU), with experience in mathematical modelling, numerical methods, scientific computing, and financial-instrument valuation.

During my internship at KPMG Deutschland, I worked on the valuation and analysis of equities, fixed-income securities, derivatives, funds, commodities, and structured products. My responsibilities included financial-market data processing, fair-value assessment, and structured valuation analysis.

My master's thesis investigated the existence of weak solutions for a variable-density, two-phase flow model. This research involved nonlinear partial differential equations (PDEs), Faedo–Galerkin approximations, energy estimates, and compactness methods.

I am particularly interested in applying mathematical and computational techniques to interdisciplinary research problems, including mathematical biology, computational modelling of multicellular systems, and the numerical simulation of complex biological processes.

## 🚀 Featured Projects
### 🧬 Cellular Automaton Model of Tumour Growth and Spatial Spread

Developed a stochastic cellular automaton in Python to investigate how cell proliferation and migration influence population size and spatial spread in a simplified two-dimensional lattice model.

**Highlights**

- Implemented probabilistic cell division and migration rules using a Moore neighbourhood.
- Designed four computational experiments to investigate migration, proliferation, their combined effects, and sensitivity to an implementation choice.
- Analysed simulation outcomes using repeated stochastic runs, summary statistics, and visualisations.
- Investigated how migration can increase spatial spread independently of population growth when cell division is disabled.
- Evaluated the sensitivity of model outcomes to the treatment of recently vacated lattice sites.
- Documented modelling assumptions, reproducibility procedures, and limitations.

**Technologies:** Python, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, Stochastic Simulation, Mathematical Modelling, Computational Biology

**Research relevance:** Mathematical Biology, Cellular Dynamics, Tumour Modelling, Scientific Computing

**Scope:** Exploratory computational model; not biologically validated.

[View repository](https://github.com/rosin21/Tumour_cellular_automaton_project/blob/main/tumour_cellular_automaton_project.ipynb))
### 🧠 Neural Networks for Solving Ordinary Differential Equations

Investigated feedforward neural networks as function approximators for
ordinary differential equations using a residual-based loss function.

**Highlights**

- Incorporated the governing differential equation and initial condition
  into the learning objective
- Trained network parameters using gradient descent
- Compared the neural-network approximation with an analytical solution
- Prepared a technical project portfolio explaining the methodology

**Topics:** Neural Networks, Gradient Descent, Numerical Methods,
Scientific Machine Learning
