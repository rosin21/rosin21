## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am an M.Sc. candidate in Computational and Applied Mathematics at
FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg, with experience in financial-instrument valuation,
mathematical modelling, numerical methods and scientific computing.

During my internship at KPMG Deutschland, I worked on the valuation and
analysis of equities, fixed-income securities, derivatives, funds,
commodities and structured products. My work included financial-market
data processing, fair-value assessment and structured valuation analysis.

My master's thesis investigates an existence result for weak solutions
of a variable-density two-phase flow model. The research involves
partial differential equations, Faedo-Galerkin approximations,
energy estimates and compactness methods.

My interests include:

- Financial and quantitative analysis
- Mathematical modelling and numerical simulation
- Machine learning for scientific applications
- Risk analysis and valuation
- High-performance and parallel computing

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 Neural Networks for Solving Ordinary Differential Equations

Investigated feedforward neural networks as function approximators for
ordinary differential equations using a residual-based loss function.

**Highlights**

- Incorporated the governing differential equation and initial condition
  into the learning objective
- Trained network parameters using gradient descent
- Compared the neural-network approximation with an analytical solution
- Prepared a technical project portfolio explaining the methodology

**Topics:** Neural Networks, Gradient Descent, Numerical Methods,
Scientific Machine Learning

[View repository](https://github.com/rosin21/neural-ode-solver)

---

### 🌡️ High-Performance Simulation of the 2D Heat Equation

Developed and optimized parallel Conjugate Gradient and Preconditioned
Conjugate Gradient solvers for a steady-state heat equation.

**Highlights**

- Parallelized computational kernels using OpenMP
- Applied LIKWID profiling and Roofline performance analysis
- Investigated cache blocking, memory bandwidth and NUMA placement
- Evaluated multicore performance on a 72-core Intel Ice Lake system

**Technologies:** C++, OpenMP, LIKWID, Linux, Numerical Methods

---

### 🛰️ Satellite Orbit Simulation under Perturbations

Modelled satellite dynamics under atmospheric drag, solar radiation
pressure, zonal harmonics and third-body gravitational effects.

**Highlights**

- Used Gauss's planetary equations and equinoctial coordinates
- Implemented RK4 and Adams-Bashforth-Moulton methods
- Analysed the effect of perturbations on long-term orbital behaviour

**Technologies:** Python, Numerical Integration, ODEs, Scientific Computing

---

### ⚓ Marine Liability Risk and Pricing Analysis

Analysed marine-liability insurance claims to identify loss patterns,
risk drivers and pricing considerations.

**Highlights**

- Analysed more than 100 insurance claims
- Applied experience-based pricing methods
- Created structured analysis and data visualisations
- Summarised findings for risk-assessment decisions

**Technologies:** Excel, Statistical Analysis, Data Visualisation,
Risk Assessment
